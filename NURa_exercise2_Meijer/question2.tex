\subsection{Simple configuration (question 2a)}

First consider an HII region that is heated only by photoionization
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:heating}
\Gamma_\mathrm{pe} = \alpha_\mathrm{B} n_\mathrm{H} n_\mathrm{e} \psi k_\mathrm{B} T_\mathrm{c},
\end{equation}
and cooled only by radiative recombination
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:cooling}
\begin{split}
\Lambda_\mathrm{rr} &= \alpha_\mathrm{B} n_\mathrm{H} n_\mathrm{e} \langle E_\mathrm{rr} \rangle \\
&= \alpha_\mathrm{B} n_\mathrm{H} n_\mathrm{e} \left[ 0.684 - 0.0416 \ln\left(\frac{T_4}{Z^2}\right) \right] k_\mathrm{B} T,
\end{split}
\end{equation}
where $Z=0.015$, $T_4 \equiv T/(10^4\,\mathrm{K})$.


The medium is in equilibrium when $\Gamma_\mathrm{pe} - \Lambda_\mathrm{rr} = 0$, so to find the equilibrium temperature, solve
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\psi T_\mathrm{c} - \left[ 0.684 - 0.0416 \ln\left(\frac{T_4}{Z^2}\right) \right] T = 0
\end{split}
\end{equation}
for T. Assume $n_e = n_\mathrm{H}$ (fully ionized gas).

The roots found by bisection and false position are within $0.1\,\mathrm{K}$ of each other.
False position needed fewer steps than bisection, and also less time.
